Your tools

Three ways to keep it going

And you? / How about you?

3

Really? How / Why / When...?

"Really? When did you start?"

How to play

The rules

  1. Work with a partner. Student A starts the conversation.
  2. Talk about the topic on the screen for 2 minutes.
  3. Use the three tools. No one-word answers!
  4. Silence for 5 seconds? Start again from the beginning.
  5. When the time is up, end the conversation politely. Then change partners.
